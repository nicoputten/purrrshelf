# Changelog

## [1.6.0](https://github.com/nicoputten/purrrshelf/compare/mobile-v1.5.0...mobile-v1.6.0) (2026-11-09)


### New tricks

* For you: suggestions under the search bar, like the next book in your series or more from authors you read
* Each suggestion says why it was picked, and a book your partner already has joins your shelf in one tap, no download needed
* The Library shows book titles and sorts by title, author or series

## [1.5.0](https://github.com/nicoputten/purrrshelf/compare/mobile-v1.4.0...mobile-v1.5.0) (2026-11-09)


### New tricks

* Settings is tidier: the Calibre import did its job and curled up for good. Your books stay right where they are

## [1.4.0](https://github.com/nicoputten/purrrshelf/compare/mobile-v1.3.0...mobile-v1.4.0) (2026-11-09)


### New tricks

* The Library filters fit in two tidy rows: whose books, and a menu for recent, unread, on my Kobo and series


### Fixed scratches

* Every book you are reading gets its own progress bar, not just a lucky few

## [1.3.0](https://github.com/nicoputten/purrrshelf/compare/mobile-v1.2.2...mobile-v1.3.0) (2026-11-09)


### New tricks

* Updates now install themselves when you leave the app, no Install screen after the first one
* New switch in Settings: install updates automatically (on, purring along by default)

## [1.2.2](https://github.com/nicoputten/purrrshelf/compare/mobile-v1.2.1...mobile-v1.2.2) (2026-11-09)


### Fixed scratches

* Check for updates spots a new version within minutes and shows when it last looked
* Update notes are short and readable, without the developer clutter
* Swat and Delivered now play where you can actually see them
* Bigger ears, a floating heart and a longer "Got it!", so the little celebrations are easier to catch
