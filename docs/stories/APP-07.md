## Story

As a reader, I want to browse my books, my partner's and all books,
so that I can see what we have and borrow from the other's shelf.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tabs: Mine / Partner's / All with counts
- [ ] Sort and filters: recently added, unread, on my Kobo, series
- [ ] Cover grid with Kobo and "Both" badges and reading progress
- [ ] Library is readable offline
